Email map

What we send, when, and why. For reviewing message content and send triggers before anything ships.

Ground rules for every email

  • One email, one purpose, one clear action.
  • Always state a real timeframe. Never "soon."
  • One short line on data privacy, not a policy page.
  • Never more than one email per state change. Merge, do not stack.
  • Same sender name every time, so it is recognizable.

Path B

Sign up → onboarding

No wait, so no onboarding email beyond the shared welcome and verify step above.

Nothing past the welcome email

Sign Up leads straight into onboarding inside the product, so there is no waiting period to fill. Past the shared welcome and verify step, the onboarding screens carry the confirmation and next-step messaging themselves: the time estimate, phase progress, and a review step before submitting.

Error and rejection screens inside the product itself (an expired invite link, a failed upload) are intentionally out of scope for this pass. This is just the email layer.
